*DJI GO 4 is a drone flight control application for Windows that connects to compatible DJI drones and puts live video, telemetry, and camera settings on one screen.*

DJI GO 4 for Windows exists to give pilots a clear, full-size view of what their drone sees and does in real time. Rather than squinting at a phone screen mid-flight, pilots get the live camera feed, flight instruments, and camera dial in one window, alongside tools for planning routes and reviewing footage afterward.

## Why people use it
- `[Live Feed]` Streams the drone's camera in real time, in sync with flight movement
- `[Telemetry]` Displays altitude, distance, speed, and battery status as you fly
- `[Camera Control]` Adjusts exposure, ISO, and gimbal angle without pausing the flight
- `[Flight Modes]` Offers intelligent flight modes for tracking and automated shots
- `[Playback]` Reviews captured photos and video right after landing

## What it needs
- **OS:** Windows 10, 64-bit or later
- **Processor:** A modern 64-bit processor able to decode live HD video smoothly
- **Memory:** Enough RAM to keep the live feed and telemetry responsive
- **Storage:** Free disk space for the app plus any footage saved locally
